Hi, I'm Dónal Ó Gaora.

Welcome to the root directory of everything I build.

This is where projects leave the whiteboard and become something real.

I'm currently an electrical apprentice with ESB Networks, while spending much of my free time writing code, designing electronic circuits, managing home lab infrastructure, and working on all sorts of side projects. Whether it's software, hardware, or something in between, I enjoy solving problems, learning new technologies, and turning ideas into practical solutions that people can genuinely use.

Alongside that, I run a small 3D printing business where I design, manufacture, and sell products of my own. It's a chance to combine engineering, creativity, and hands-on problem solving while bringing ideas from concept to finished product.

Most things I build eventually find their way here. Whether you're here to browse my shop, learn more about me, or explore some of the projects I've been working on, I hope you find something interesting.
